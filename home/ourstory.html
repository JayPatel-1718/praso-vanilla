Our Story

Celebrating individuality through fashion.

The Idea for Praso

The idea for Praso sparked during a grand fashion show managed by the founders of Clique Entertainment. The event buzzed with excitement, featuring top models strutting down the runway and celebrities dazzling in the front row. The clothes were stunning, yet something felt off. The founders noticed a troubling trend: every outfit looked strikingly similar, prioritizing brand logos over the wearer’s identity. It felt like the models were merely showcasing labels, not clothes that reflected their true selves.

A Vision Born

That same night, a spark of inspiration struck one of the founders. They pondered, “What if we could create clothes that truly represent the wearer? Garments that tell personal stories, not just chase fleeting trends?” They envisioned their own designs gracing the runway—pieces that celebrated the individual, not the brand. This vision of authentic, expressive fashion became the cornerstone of what would soon be Praso.

Building Praso

Fueled by excitement, the founders wasted no time. They assembled a passionate team and crafted a handful of unique designs that broke away from the norm. These initial pieces were tested with friends and family, earning enthusiastic feedback. Encouraged, they launched Praso with a clear mission: to create clothing that empowers self-expression. To make the brand even more personal, they introduced a feature allowing customers to upload their own designs and share their stories, fostering a community of creativity.

Praso Today

Today, Praso stands as a beacon of individuality. More than just a clothing brand, it’s a platform where everyone—models, celebrities, or everyday people—can wear what feels authentic to them. Praso’s collections and custom design feature invite everyone to express who they are through fashion. As the brand grows, its core mission remains unchanged: to craft clothes that let people shine as their true selves.
